Model & prices

Cover estimates use the existing pregame projected margin, a normal approximation, and the rolling backtest margin RMSE for the league and period. These probabilities are model estimates awaiting calibration. For whole-point lines, the display adjusts break-even for the estimated push probability. Archived grades use the saved line and the verified period score.

First-half projections appear when available. First-half cover and edge require published first-half spread and price for that same quote.
